# Public aggregate data

These CSV files transcribe selected aggregate values from internal studies supplied by Daniela, who authorized publication of the study data with names anonymized. Organization: Health insurer A. No original PDFs, patient records, personal identifiers, provider names, small geographical cells or employer-branded screenshots are included.

- cost_history.csv: cost study (2024), pp. 4, 7-8. Each year covers five accounting months. Costs are nominal BRL. 2009 membership is approximate. Cost-per-member values are retained as reported and may differ slightly from ratios of the displayed rounded totals.
- chronic_conditions.csv: population study (2025), p. 16. Selected categories, 2021-May 2025. Categories are non-additive; do not interpret their sum as a unique population count.
- chronic_cost_share.csv: population study, p. 27. 2024-May 2025. Reported percentages, a different observation window from the mapping chart.
- revenue_scenarios.csv: population study, p. 37. 2025 covers six remaining months. Positive loss magnitudes in BRL millions. These are rounded estimates from the original 2025 SARIMA planning exercise, not observed losses, prediction intervals or outputs of the updated 2026 SARIMAX intervention model.

Charts reconstruct reported figures. No source model has been re-run from the full internal dataset. The original inflation-adjusted series is not reproduced here because the cumulative adjustment method has not been independently reconciled.
