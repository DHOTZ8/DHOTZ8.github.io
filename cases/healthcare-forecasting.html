Healthcare Forecasting

Time-series models for planning and scenarios.

01 · Business question

How can historical trends inform healthcare planning?

I developed Python scripts for membership and financial forecasting. The work includes seasonal models, comparison of forecasting families, temporal holdout evaluation and scenarios for a changing membership trajectory.

02 · My approach

01

Membership scenarios

Model deviations from a robust historical trend with SARIMAX and a ramp intervention. Explore gradual total reductions of 3%, 5% and 10% relative to a central scenario by the horizon end.

02

Model comparison

Compare ARIMA, SARIMA, ETS and Prophet for healthcare cost, revenue and copayment series using a chronological train/test split.

03

Evaluation and diagnostics

Calculate forecast error metrics and inspect residual behavior. The membership script also compares against a seasonal naive baseline and evaluates interval coverage.

03 · Analytical view

Making the approach visible.

The chart illustrates a historical series, a forecast boundary and a widening uncertainty band. All values and the band are illustrative; they were not generated by the original models.

04 · Decision relevance

From analysis to context.

Forecasts and scenarios provide a structured input to financial and membership planning. Model selection should reflect out-of-sample evidence and the consequences of forecast error.

Interpretation & limitations

Scenario assumptions are planning choices, not probabilities. Pre-intervention validation does not demonstrate accuracy under a future structural change. No accuracy or savings claim is made without reproducible results.
